Frequently Asked Questions

How do I wash and care for my handmade knot bag?

▼
We recommend gentle spot cleaning with lukewarm water and mild organic soap. For a full refresh, hand-wash gently and lay flat on a dry towel in indirect sunlight to preserve stitch shape.

Can I request a custom color or personalized strap size?

▼
Yes, absolutely! Custom colors and personalization are welcomed at knotyourbag with no extra charge. Simply note your preferred shade, monogram, or strap dimensions in the personalization field on the order form.

How long will it take for my order to arrive?

▼
Ready-to-ship items are packaged and dispatched within 24 hours. Custom hand-knotted requests take 2-3 business days of careful crafting, followed by express doorstep delivery (usually 3-5 business days total).

What are the customer service hours?

▼
We are proud to be open 24/7, 6 days a week, 365 days a year! You can call us directly at 3319565982 or email fabianojessica6@gmail.com whenever you need assistance.
